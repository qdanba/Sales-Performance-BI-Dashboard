# Analysis

This folder contains the business analysis deliverable for the **Sales Performance & BI Dashboard** project.

## Deliverable
- `Sales_Performance_Insight_Analysis_Bilingual.docx` – bilingual Vietnamese and English interpretation of the dashboard findings.

## Analysis scope
1. **Sales Overview** – overall sales performance, trends, revenue mix, and the unusual sales pattern at the end of FY2025.
2. **Product Performance** – sales contribution by product category and leading products.
3. **Customer Analysis** – Store vs. Individual customer mix, revenue contribution, and key customers.
4. **Territory Analysis** – sales distribution across countries and sales territories.

## Interpretation approach
Each insight is structured as:
- **Finding:** what the data shows.
- **Insight:** what the observed pattern may mean.
- **Business implication:** a potential area for management attention or further investigation.

## Data limitations
- The analysis uses the AdventureWorks2025 sample database; it does not represent a real operating company.
- No Bikes or Components transactions are recorded in June 2025. The data does not establish the cause.
- FY2022 is partial (May–June 2022), so comparisons against it require caution.
- Territory analysis is limited to the geographic attributes available in the source data.
